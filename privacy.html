Hunt Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Hunt may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, configuration settings, and moderation or logging records when needed to provide its features. The exact data depends on how each server configures and uses the bot.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide moderation, security, logging, verification, ticket management, and other requested features.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the bot.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the bot's features and operational requirements. For data questions or deletion requests, contact the Hunt support team.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect stored information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Hunt support team through the official support server.
